In this world, every pet writes a REVIEW of its owner, and it gets posted on your front door.

Every Saturday. Stars and everything. The dog next door gives five stars every week. "He dropped a meatball in 2021. I think about it daily." My cousin's goldfish wrote, "Three stars. Who is this guy." My aunt's parrot just posted her phone password. But five stars has a price. Mrs. Garza got five stars once. Now she has twenty-three cats, a goat, and a waiting list.

My cat is named Dennis. Dennis has given me one star eight Saturdays in a row. "ONE STAR. He sings in the shower." "One star. Touched my belly in 2022. I remember." "One star. Gets home at 3:42. I wait at the window from 3:30. For nothing."

Here's the problem. Twelve one-star reviews in a row, and you LOSE your pet. The Pet Office moves it to a better home. I had four Saturdays left.

So I tried to buy a star. Week nine, I got the fancy salmon in the tiny gold can. Fourteen dollars. "One star. Salmon was fine. Service was slow." Week ten, I built a six-foot cat tower with four floors. "One star. I slept in the box. The box is drafty." Week eleven, I gave him my whole bed. I slept on the floor for seven nights. "HALF a star. He snores down there too." I didn't know half a star existed. Dennis found a way.

Friday night, I quit. No salmon. No tower. I sat on the floor next to his box. "Okay. You WIN. If you want a better home, I'll carry your box there myself. But you're the first thing I look for when I open the door. Every single day." Then I cried on him a little. He allowed it for four seconds.

Saturday morning, the review was on the door. I read it with one eye closed. "TWO stars. He cried on me. Disgusting. I'm STAYING. To every pet reading this door: he is TAKEN. Why do you think I kept him at one star?" I looked across the street. Twenty-three cats and a goat were staring at my door.

Sunday, the goat was on my porch. Dennis handled it. Then he posted a second review. About the goat. ZERO stars.
